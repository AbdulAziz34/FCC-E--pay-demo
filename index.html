About FCC E-pay


FCC E-pay is a student demo project inspired by the Freetown City Council's "Transform Freetown" agenda to create a vibrant, inclusive city for all. This platform was designed to show how residents could easily pay for city services like local tax, market dues, and other council rates using mobile money- Orange Money and Afrimoney- from their phone, without visiting the council office.

Our Mission (Demo Concept)

To make civic payments faster, transparent, and accessible for every for every Freetowner, supporting efficient and people-centered services delivery.
